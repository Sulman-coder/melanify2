A collection for two hosts

Since MELANIFY requires two hosts, our parts collection reflects that. One secretion mechanism in Pichia pastoris produces the thermostable HotPETase, which degrades PET into terephthalic acid and MHET. These compounds are transported to catechol, the precursor of allomelanin, in Escherichia coli by a sequence of cassettes. Hydrolysis is finished by the cell surface-displayed MHETase. Terephthalic acid is converted to protocatechuic acid by the four tph genes of Comamonas sp. E6, and then to catechol by AroY.

Prior to synthesis, we optimized each codon for its target host and checked it against its DNA and protein references in UniProt or NCBI. Part numbers will be assigned on submission.
