Our project timeline

  1. PROJECT CONSOLIDATION

    Refining the idea

    Expert discussions helped move the project from plastic degradation toward upcycling and a locally relevant application.

  2. MARCH 2026

    Introducing HotPETase to younger students

    “The Journey of the Hero HotPETase” introduced primary students to proteins, plastic, and the possibilities of synthetic biology.

  3. RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

    Design, build, test, learn

    Our laboratory work connected enzyme design, construct validation, and testing across the two host organisms.

  4. STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT

    Learning from industry and UNIDO

    Conversations about feedstock quality, scale, safety, and alternative products shaped our implementation plans.

  5. OCTOBER 2026

    Sharing the project nationally

    Thai media engagement helped communicate the project’s local context and its current research stage to a wider audience.

  6. NOVEMBER 2026 · PLANNED

    iGEM Grand Jamboree

    The team hopes to represent the school at the iGEM Grand Jamboree in Paris.

Acknowledgements

We thank the researchers, recycling industry representatives, community survey participants, parents, and students who shared their perspectives. Their feedback helped us examine the project beyond the laboratory.
