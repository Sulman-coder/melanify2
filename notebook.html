Method 1.​ Incubate the purified plasmid at approximately 80 C for 30 minutes with the tube cap open, allowing water to evaporate. 2.​ Dilute 0.5 uL of the concentrated plasmid with 1.5 uL UP water, a 4-fold dilution. 3.​ Measure on the NanoDrop. 4.​ Multiply the reading by 4 to obtain the true concentration.
