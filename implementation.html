From research to application

Our long-term goal is to have MELANIFY serve as a platform that converts plastic waste into a melanin-based ingredient for sun-care products. The proposed route connects recovered PET, a dual-host bioconversion pathway, and isolated allomelanin for sunscreen and cosmetics formulators.

PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION

MELANIFY remains in development. We have not yet manufactured a final sunscreen formulation or confirmed its safety for human use. Safety, stability, UV performance, and regulatory compliance must be established before real-world use.

A connected production pathway

  1. 01

    Source and prepare recovered PET

    Work with existing waste aggregators and recycling networks. Sorting, washing, and mechanical or thermal pre-treatment prepare the feedstock for biological cleavage. Stakeholder feedback led us to test commercial, highly crystalline PET to better represent real recycling streams.

  2. 02

    Break the polymer into monomers

    Pichia pastoris provides extracellular HotPETase, while surface-displayed MHETase in Escherichia coli completes hydrolysis of MHET to terephthalic acid and ethylene glycol.

  3. 03

    Convert recovered carbon to catechol

    The tph pathway converts terephthalic acid to protocatechuic acid. AroY, supported by EcdB and EcdD, then provides the catechol precursor for polymerization. Individual stages are described in our engineering cycles.

  4. 04

    Harvest an isolated biomaterial

    Catechol undergoes oxidative polymerization under open-air, slightly basic conditions to form allomelanin. The intended final material is isolated from the production organisms. Separation and purification must be validated as the process develops.

An ingredient for formulators

Our proposed business model supplies pigment by the kilogram to sunscreen and cosmetics formulators. We position allomelanin as a cosmetic bio-active with potential roles as a UV-booster, photostabiliser, antioxidant, and natural colorant. These are development targets requiring substantiation.

What must happen next

PROCESS

Demonstrate a reliable pathway

Optimize conversion, enzyme stability, yield, feedstock consistency, and scalable cell-free harvesting.

VALIDATION

Build the evidence

Test the isolated ingredient and final formulations for safety, stability, UV performance, and environmental impact.

COLLABORATION

Develop with partners

Work with formulators, recycling stakeholders, and relevant authorities before moving toward a marketable product.

Consultation with UNIDO also encouraged us to view MELANIFY as a flexible platform. Different regions may value different products: stopping at terephthalic acid or exploring other PET-derived compounds could allow the pathway to respond to local needs.
